User account requests

Account Deletion

Beyond Grades users can request deletion of their account and associated data by email.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ijroy037@gmail.com from the email address used for your Beyond Grades account. Use the subject Beyond Grades account deletion and include the account email or sign-in identifier you want deleted.

What will be deleted

After ownership is verified, the request will cover the Beyond Grades account profile and associated profile data, including name, profile photo, college or university details, course, year, date of birth, phone number, bio, event participation, feedback, ratings, and comments held for the account.

Review and processing

Requests are reviewed and processed by IJ Roy. Additional verification may be requested to prevent unauthorized deletion. Some records may be retained where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Deletion contact: ijroy037@gmail.com
