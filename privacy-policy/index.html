Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Beyond Grades, developed by IJ Roy, handles information connected with the Beyond Grades app and website.

Information collected

Beyond Grades may collect account information needed to sign in and operate the app, including email address, authentication identifiers, name, and profile information provided through Google Sign-In, Microsoft Sign-In, or Firebase Authentication.

Users may provide college or university, course, year, date of birth, phone number, bio, and profile photo. The app may also process event participation, teammate feedback, ratings, and optional comments.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, maintain profiles, show relevant university events, support feedback flows, calculate growth indicators, provide support, protect the service, and keep the app working.

Service providers

Beyond Grades uses Firebase services and its backend infrastructure to provide authentication, data storage, and app functionality. Beyond Grades does not sell user data or share it for advertising.

Security

Data sent by the app is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are used for the service.

Account deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and associated data through the Account Deletion page.
