Your growth profile

Make the work outside the classroom visible.

University life is full of things that do not fit into a marksheet. Beyond Grades gives your events, contributions, and feedback a place to build a clearer picture of how you are growing.

Keep your profile current, discover what is happening around you, and use teammate feedback to understand the skills you bring to a team.

Inside the app

Everything that helps your next step feel clearer.

01

Explore events

When your university publishes events, they show up in one place with the details you need to decide where to go next.

02

Complete your profile

Add your name, college, course, year, photo, bio, and other details that help your profile reflect you.

03

Give feedback

Rate your teammate’s contribution across the skills configured for an event and leave an optional comment.

04

See your snapshot

Use your growth snapshot to see event participation, top skills, and evaluation signals as the experience grows.
